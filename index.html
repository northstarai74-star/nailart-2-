01 — About the Studio

Precision first.
Art always.

Founder & Lead Artist — Dasha Kim

LUMÉ began as a single chair in a Toronto apartment and grew into a studio and teaching space built on one idea: nail art is a craft, not a trend — and it deserves to be taught the way any serious craft is taught.

Every client set is hand‑painted or hand‑sculpted to order. Every student in the academy leaves with technique they can defend — structure, ratio, cure, sanitation — not just a pretty photo. We work in small numbers on purpose.

06 — Good to Know

Questions,
answered.

Do I need experience for the Foundation course?

No — Fundamentals assumes zero prior experience. You'll leave with the base technique needed for the Sculpting course, if you choose to continue.

Can I bring my own reference photo for a client appointment?

Always. We'll adapt any reference to your nail shape, length and lifestyle, or design something original with you in‑chair.

What's your cancellation policy?

48 hours' notice for a full refund or reschedule. Inside 48 hours, a 50% fee applies to cover the reserved seat.

Do academy students get ongoing support after the course?

Yes — every graduate joins our alumni channel for critique, referrals, and continuing workshops.
